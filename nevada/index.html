Background

In November of 2019, the Governor of Nevada, Steve Sisolak signed Executive Order 2019-22, directing state agencies to develop a comprehensive state climate strategy, to include the reduction of carbon emissions from electricity, transportation, buildings, state operations, and other sectors. NZero submitted a bid to the state's RFP, and was awarded the contract in partnership with Deloitte. NZero was to provide carbon tracking services across all state owned buildings, and with this energy & emissions data, Deloitte was to provide departmental analysis in addition to a statewide capital planning report.

The challenge

The relevant data had to be sourced across 13 agencies, each with its own systems and data owners. I joined shortly after kickoff to manage data ingestion: tracking what each agency sent, and working with data teams to ensure data was complete and accurate post-processing. This helped ensure that the data being provided to Deloitte for analysis was reliable.

Results

Onboarding finished in roughly nine months, and took a team of nearly 25 people. In the end, the team (NZero & Deloitte) had delivered:

  • 453 sites reporting energy, water and emissions across 13 departments.
  • 16.9 million square feet — 100% of the state square footage consuming energy.
  • 47 state employees trained to manage energy & emissions through the platform.
  • A statewide capital planning report, built on that baseline alongside Deloitte’s departmental analysis.
