Distilling AI adoption through operator interviews

Benchmarking AI Adoption

Role:
Researcher

16 interviews

on experience with AI to-date & workflow opportunities

38 use cases

documented across 9 firms

The goal of this project is to map AI adoption through operator interviews. Specifically, where different industries/segments fall on the adoption curve, how AI is being used in each industry, and the opportunities for AI native companies to disrupt industries through new business models. The interviews span operators across a range of functions, seniority levels, industries, and organization sizes.

Until the sample size is great enough for broader analysis, this page will only include anonymized firm-level performance and use cases.

Initial adoption tracker

Finance Manufacturing Real estate Energy Retail Non-profit Technology Construction Other

Method

Adoption depth (0–4). 0: no use. 1: a few people use it ad hoc. 2: most staff use it weekly for general tasks. 3: at least one core workflow runs through AI every time. 4: a workflow was rebuilt around AI with a measured result.

Each dot is a firm, scored from one interview. At larger organizations, the score covers the part of the firm the interviewee can see, such as their team or division.
