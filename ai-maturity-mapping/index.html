Working at an AI company, I've seen firsthand the value of embedding AI in a business (especially when driven by pressure). However, in talking with people across other industries, adoption and value realization is significantly behind, despite immediate gains being achievable with a basic subscription. My goal with this project is to better understand what the adoption curve across industries looks like, who will change first, how they will change, and how AI native companies will transform industries (especially professional services) through new business models.

So far, I've had the chance to interview people in the following industries:

  • Finance: Accounting, Investment Banking, Wealth Management
  • Manufacturing: Hardware, PCB (Printed Circuit Boards), Industrial Services
  • Real Estate: Commercial, Residential, Property Management
  • Energy: Regulation & Policy
  • Retail: Home Improvement, Digital Advertising
  • Non-Profit: Religious Organizations
  • Technology: SaaS

I'm continuing to interview people, and will update this page with learnings once I have a final work product. Reach out to me at evan.ferguson0@gmail.com if you would like to share your perspective.
